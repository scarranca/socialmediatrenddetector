# trend-detector

## What it is
A local dashboard for a solo founder/creator that shows which short-form video patterns are getting reach in their industry (scraped via Apify), and hands them ready-to-shoot scripts written for their profile.

## Who uses it, where
A solo founder or creator, at their desk in daylight, once or twice a week, before planning the week's content. They're scanning for "what should I shoot" — not monitoring live metrics. Light theme: it sits next to a Notion page and a camera app, not a terminal.

## Register
product — a tool. Familiar analytics-dashboard grammar (Linear/Stripe-grade), light canvas, white cards, one green accent for "this is working", amber for "peaking", neutral for "saturated".

## Platform
web (local, desktop-first, ≥1024px primary; degrades to single column)

## Jobs
1. See at a glance whether the sample is healthy (videos, views, engagement, outliers).
2. Pick the trend to ride this week — ranked by fit, with momentum and evidence.
3. Read/copy the script for that trend (hook, beats, caption, hashtags).
4. Re-run the pipeline and watch it happen.

## Design reference
User-supplied mock: light gray canvas, white 20px-radius cards, big numerals with small green delta pills, stepped dotted-gradient bar chart with a floating stat card, left icon rail, pill tabs, compact bar/wave sparklines. Inter-style geometric sans, one family.
